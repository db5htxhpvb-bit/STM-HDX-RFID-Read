JLCPCB DFM Notes and Recommended Order Options

Board options (as requested):
- Board: 4-layer, 1.6 mm thickness (default)
- Copper weight: 1 oz (as specified). Recommend upgrade to 2 oz for production if budget allows.
- Surface finish: ENIG recommended for assembly reliability, HASL acceptable for prototypes.
- Solder mask: Green default
- Silkscreen: Top and bottom

Via and hole recommendations:
- Via drill minimum: 0.3 mm (JLCPCB standard). Use 0.3 mm tented vias for thermal vias if possible.
- Thermal vias: 12 under each MOSFET exposed pad; ensure via annular ring meets JLC spec.

Assembly notes:
- BOM and centroid prepared for JLC assembly upload. Common passives set to "JLC优先采购" where possible; large/power parts listed as "客户自备".
- For parts not in JLC inventory mark as 'Customer Supplied' when ordering assembly.

DFM checks before upload:
- Ensure clearance >= 6 mil for tracks/space. Confirm footprints and paste masks.
- Check tenting and paste openings for thermal pads to avoid tombstoning.
